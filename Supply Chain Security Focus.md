## Supply Chain Security Focus

Modern AI systems rely heavily on external dependencies, containers, and third-party libraries.

As part of this project, you will analyze:
- A provided Dockerfile
- A container vulnerability scan (`06_trivy_report.json`)

You will:
- Identify critical and high vulnerabilities
- Assess risk to the ML pipeline
- Recommend remediation actions

This simulates real-world AI security risks where infrastructure weaknesses can undermine otherwise secure models.
